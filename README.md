# SkillXchange

**Learn a skill. Teach a skill. Grow together.**

SkillXchange is a peer-to-peer skill exchange platform that connects students who want to learn new skills with students who are willing to teach them. It creates a collaborative learning environment where everyone can be both a mentor and a learner.

Instead of relying only on traditional courses, SkillXchange encourages students to share their knowledge, exchange skills, and learn from one another.

## Problem Statement

Many students possess valuable skills but lack opportunities to share them. At the same time, other students want to learn new skills but may not have access to affordable mentorship.

Traditional learning platforms often focus on one-way learning, where students consume content without actively contributing.

SkillXchange aims to solve this problem by creating a community-driven learning ecosystem where knowledge becomes a shared resource.

## Key Features

### Current Prototype

- **Skill Discovery:** Explore skills that other students can teach.
- **Teach and Learn:** Users can identify skills they want to teach and skills they want to learn.
- **Peer-to-Peer Learning:** Connect learners with potential mentors.
- **Responsive Interface:** Access the platform through a responsive React-based frontend.
- **Local Data Persistence:** Uses browser Local Storage to simulate user data and application state.

### Planned Features

- **Smart Mentor Matching:** AI-powered recommendations based on skills, interests, and learning goals.
- **Skill Exchange Credits:** Earn credits by teaching others and use them to receive mentorship.
- **Expert Mentorship:** Redeem earned credits for guidance from professors and industry experts.
- **Video Mentoring:** Conduct online mentoring sessions.
- **Real-Time Chat:** Communicate with mentors and learners.
- **Progress Tracking:** Track learning progress and completed sessions.
- **Notifications:** Receive updates about sessions, requests, and learning activities.

## How It Works

1. Create a profile and identify the skills you can teach.
2. Add the skills you want to learn.
3. Discover students with matching skills.
4. Connect with potential mentors or learners.
5. Schedule a learning session.
6. Share knowledge and grow together.

In SkillXchange, a student is not limited to being a learner or a teacher. Every user can contribute knowledge and benefit from the knowledge of others.

## Reward System

SkillXchange introduces a contribution-based learning model.

Students earn credits by sharing their knowledge and teaching other students. These credits can be used to access mentorship from professors and industry experts.

This model encourages active participation and makes quality mentorship more accessible.
